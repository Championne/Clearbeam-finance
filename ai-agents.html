Services

Small, practical steps with AI agents

Three ways to start, kept deliberately small. These describe what I offer; they are not results from earlier client work.

01

Process review for automation

We look at one finance process together, such as month-end, the budget submission cycle or the monthly reporting pack, and map which steps an AI agent could prepare, which need human judgement, and where the controls sit.

  • A short written assessment of the process
  • Candidate steps for an AI agent, ranked by effort and risk
  • Data-protection and control points to resolve first
02

Small, controlled pilots

A time-boxed pilot on one contained task, run alongside your existing process so nothing depends on it until it has proved itself. Typical candidates:

  • Drafting variance commentary from actuals against budget and reforecast
  • Reconciliation matching and exception lists for review
  • Collecting and checking budget inputs; assembling reporting packs
03

AI alongside your finance team

Combined with an interim or consultancy finance role: I bring AI agents into the day-to-day work where they help, and document how they are used so your team can run them without me.

  • Practical use within the tools your IT already approves
  • Clear review steps and documentation
  • Handover, or a clear "this does not help here"
Approach

How I work with AI agents

  1. Understand the process first

    Who owns it, what the inputs are, which checks matter and what the timetable is, before any tool is chosen.

  2. Keep a person in control

    An agent prepares, a person reviews and signs off. Every output can be traced back to its source data.

  3. Protect the data

    Agree up front what data the agent may see, where it is processed and which tools your IT and data-protection rules allow (GDPR).

  4. Pilot small, decide honestly

    Agree what a useful result looks like before starting, run the pilot in parallel, and then scale it, adjust it or stop.
