Case studies in the insurance industry

Three pieces of work from my finance roles in the insurance industry. The descriptions come from my CV and the figures from my time in those roles.

Europe · Change project

Leading the ARCS reconciliation rollout

Accountant Benelux, Lisbon

As part of the company-wide Finance Transformation Programme and the move from Sun to Oracle, I led the implementation of Oracle ARCS automated reconciliations for the European businesses: the Netherlands, Belgium, France, Germany, Spain and Portugal, and later Ireland. On the premium bank account, which handled thousands of transactions a month, ARCS matched our cash flow bookings against the bank statements using automatic rules. The majority of transactions were allocated automatically, and only a small part still needed manual allocation. That made the reconciliation much faster and more consistent.

Seven countries all using the same automatic reconciliation process.
