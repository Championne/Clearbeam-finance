Services

Lead a finance function, or strengthen the team around your finance lead

Four ways to work together. On an interim basis I can own a function myself, below CFO level, or support your CFO, Finance Director or Head of FP&A. Everything here builds on work I have done in the role.

01

Interim country finance manager or head of controlling

When the seat itself is empty, I step in and run the function, not just support it: the finance of a country or branch entity, or the controlling team, reporting to your CFO or group finance. I do not offer interim CFO roles; I focus on the functions below that level that I can own fully. If you need cover rather than a new owner, I can also step into an FP&A or controlling role during a vacancy, absence, restructuring or busy period, supporting your finance lead and keeping the cycle on track.

  • Own the budget, reforecast and expense planning for the entity
  • Monthly control of actuals against budget and reforecast, variance analysis, accruals and month-end
  • Budget conversations with cost-centre heads about current and future plans
  • Reporting to group finance, the CFO or Finance Director and the Board
  • Keeping accounts payable, treasury and local declarations under control
  • Steady leadership through a vacancy, restructuring or transition

Built on preparing the entire budget and reforecast expense planning for Hiscox Germany, supervising accounts payable and treasury for the Benelux, and working closely with the Finance Director and country Finance Managers.

02

Budgeting & forecasting cycles

Run or support an annual budget, reforecast or multi-year plan, from preparation and cost-centre challenge through to Board-level reporting.

  • Annual budgets, reforecasts and business plans
  • Multi-year (e.g. 3-year) budget plans, including in Oracle planning
  • Challenging and analysing business partners' budget inputs
03

Business partnering & fractional support

A senior finance partner for your Finance Director or CFO, part-time or for a set number of days a week.

  • Budget conversations with cost-centre heads about current and future plans
  • Preparation for strategic initiatives, meetings and presentations
  • Day-to-day support in running a finance department
04

Finance change projects

Projects and hands-on delivery when finance is changing, from shaping the change to doing the work until it runs.

  • Organisational restructuring and expense-ratio programmes
  • Process redesign: streamlining budget and reforecast submissions and reconciliations
  • ERP and system changes: the finance side of rollouts, getting budgets, reconciliations and reporting working in the new set-up (Oracle ERP and Planning experience)
  • Cleanup and remediation: bringing reconciliations, accruals and expense control back in order
  • Multi-entity and multi-currency consolidation and reporting across European operations

Engagements can be interim (full- or part-time), fractional, or project-based, and run remotely from Lisbon with on-site visits when needed. Terms are discussed per engagement. Interested in AI agents for finance processes? See AI agents for finance.

Approach

I run the finance cycle end to end. At Hiscox Germany I prepared the entire budget and reforecast expense planning and controlled actual expenses against it, working directly with cost-centre heads on their current and future plans.
